## Installation & Flashing Instructions

### Prerequisites
1. An unlocked bootloader on your Redmi 9A.
2. ADB and Fastboot drivers installed on your PC.
3. A custom recovery (TWRP or OrangeFox) flashed to your device.

### Flashing a Custom ROM / GSI
1. Boot into custom recovery (`Volume Up + Power`).
2. Wipe `Data`, `Cache`, `System`, and `Vendor`.
3. Flash your preferred ROM or GSI image to the `System` partition.
4. (Optional) Flash Google Apps (NikGApps / BitGApps) if using a vanilla build.
5. Format Data (`Yes`) to prevent bootloops caused by encryption.
6. Reboot to System.
